About Me

I am a graduate researcher in Computer Science at Keio University (HVRL) and Ecole Centrale de Nantes, with a theoretical ML foundation from PSL University (M2 IASD). Research aspirations center on developing Computer Vision methods for medical image analysis, with particular interests in unsupervised and representation learning for domain adaptation, anomaly detection, and segmentation, while enthusiastic about exploring the broader spectrum of Computer Vision challenges for medical applications.
